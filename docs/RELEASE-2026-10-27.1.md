# Release 2026-10-27.1 — the header, tidied

Three small things in the header, all of them because it was looked at
properly: the datalogger's model text goes, the day of the week comes
back, and the cog gets big enough to hit.

## The datalogger's model text goes

The header said:

```
Your plant  1B2C3D4E5F60   [LIVE] [0.2s] [Tue 27 Oct 2026 · 10:18] [⚙]
```

`WLan-WW (WiFi & LAN)` is the model of the data logger. It is worth
knowing — it is in the system panel, where there is room for it — but it
is not worth the space at the top of the screen, and the serial is what
identifies the station anyway. The header now says:

```
Your plant  1B2C3D4E5F60   [LIVE] [0.2s] [Tue 27 Oct 2026 · 10:18] [⚙]
```

## The day of the week comes back

The last release dropped the weekday on screens under 1000 px, to make
room for the date. With the datalogger's model text gone there is room
for it again, so the weekday is on every screen:

| screen | the header says |
|---|---|
| every size | `Tue 27 Oct 2026 · 10:18` |

The plant details still go before the plant name on the smallest
screens, where there is no room for anything else.

## The cog

The cog at the end of the header opens the settings page, and it was
small — 23 px on the Pi screen, which is a poor thing to ask a finger
to hit. The picture is a little bigger now, and the target is bigger
than the picture:

```css
.gear::after{content:"";position:absolute;inset:-.55em;border-radius:999px}
```

On the 800×480 panel the cog is 28×25 px and the thing you actually
touch is 44×41 px. The header grows by 4 px at that size, and not at
all on a bigger screen.

## Testing

- The header was measured at six sizes with the real plant name: the
  header fits with no truncation anywhere.
- The layout check: all 48 viewports fit on one screen, no scrolling.
- 345 tests (was 342).

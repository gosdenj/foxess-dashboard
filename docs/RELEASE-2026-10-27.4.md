# Release 2026-10-27.4 — the hub says it is the hub

The inverter's ring on the Power Flow slide is a little bigger than the
four around it, and the state of charge in the middle is the biggest
number on the screen.

## What changed

The hub's ring was 60 units across, the satellites' 50 — a difference
that was there, but too slight to read from across a room. It is now 68,
so the inverter is plainly the middle of the diagram rather than one
ring among five.

The charge level went with it: 31 points against the satellites' 25, so
the one number the hub exists to show is the one you see first. The
inverter's own little wave mark grew to match.

The labels under the hub — `INVERTER` and the firmware version — moved
down by a few points, because the bigger ring now reaches further down
and they were in its way.

## The wires

The four dashed wires end exactly on the edge of the ring they join, so
growing the hub meant measuring them out again: the hub's end of each
wire moved further out, from 60 to 68, and the satellites' ends were
tidied up to the same standard.

That is not a thing to leave to the eye, so the test that checks it now
reads the radii out of the markup rather than writing them down. A
radius can change without the test going stale, and a wire that stops
reaching its ring is still caught.

## Testing

- 392 tests (was 390): the rings' geometry — that the hub is the
  bigger ring, that every wire still lands on the ring it belongs to,
  and that the hub's level is the largest number on the diagram.
- The layout check: all 48 viewports fit on one screen, no scrolling.
- The flow-direction check: 34 animated wires across 6 states and 2
  diagrams, all still pointing the way the power is going.
- The dark and light themes were both looked at.
